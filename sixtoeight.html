The BFG is a children's fantasy novel by Roald Dahl that tells the story of Sophie, a brave young orphan who is taken from her orphanage by the Big Friendly Giant, or BFG. Unlike the other giants in Giant Country, who eat humans, the BFG is kind and survives by eating a revolting vegetable called snozzcumbers. He spends his nights collecting and delivering happy dreams to children. When Sophie discovers that the other giants plan to travel to England and eat children, she and the BFG work together to stop them.
